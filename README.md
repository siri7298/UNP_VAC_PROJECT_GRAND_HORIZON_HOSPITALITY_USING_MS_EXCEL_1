# UNP_VAC_PROJECT_GRAND_HORIZON_HOSPITALITY_USING_MS_EXCEL_1

Completed a data analytics project in collaboration with UNP Education, focusing on hospitality revenue management and econometric yield analytics. Preprocessed and cleaned datasets, performed exploratory data analysis (EDA), and developed visualizations to explore relationships between key economic and business variables influencing revenue performance. Conducted statistical hypothesis testing and regression analysis to examine the impact of factors such as Average Daily Rate (ADR), OTA marketing spend, and corporate demand on revenue and RevPAR. Derived insights to support data-driven business decisions and revenue optimization.
